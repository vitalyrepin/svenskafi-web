About the event

Finnish-Swedish short film & language café in Swedish

Are you interested in Finnish-Swedish culture and would like to get to know it better?

Or maybe you are looking for a good reason to practice your Swedish?

This autumn Svenska.fi and Globe Art Point in cooperation with Finlandssvensk Filmcentrum has prepared for you a course with Finnish-Swedish short films followed by language café on topics, related to the film.

Host : freelance journalist Marcus Floman
