About the event

Hi all and welcome to DAD & ME : a meeting point for dads with their kids.  

Our goal is to make us dads remain in our kids' memory as active parents through doing things together!  We offer handcrafts, drawing, easy language exercises, and free space for your own interest and fantasy. Another important part of our idea is to create a space for meeting with other dads, have a cup of tea or coffee, share experiences and just have a fun on Sunday.  

We have prepared some program for our events but we are also flexible to adjust. The usual event language is English but we also speak Swedish, Russian, Arabic, German and some other languages ;)  

Recommended age : 2-8 years
