Om evenemanget

Hello dear parents! (Swedish text below)

Welcome to Swedish Sundays!

Svenska.fi invite you to spend a good time in Swedish speaking environment making handicraft listening to songs on Swedish and have a fun together! During event kids and parents can make a bookmarks, paint or do other handicraft according to own wishes. The idea is that we try practice everything on Swedish but ofc there is no language requirments.

Recommended age: 0-10 years Babies are welcome!
