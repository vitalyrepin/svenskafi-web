Beginner course (A1.1-A2.0), 4 months

Arkiverad

The course gives basic spoken and written knowledge of the Swedish language, as well as an insight into Finnish-Swedish culture and society. Beside that you will access to professional social advising that will help you to recognise and maximise your individual benefits of learning Swedish in Finland. Every month we organize an event and we visit places related to Finnish-Swedish culture in Helsingfors(Helsinki) region. You also get a priority in participation in other activities, organized by Svenska.fi

Course structure:

- 48 hours of online classes in small group with teacher (1.5h/ 2 times a week)

- Access to materials and video recordings throughout the course

- Personal advising

- Cultural events in Svenskfinland
